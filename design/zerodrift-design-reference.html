plate 06 · webhook retries

Telling Café Aman, until they hear it

Their server was down for a while. Each retry waits twice as long as the last, plus a little random jitter, so a struggling server isn't flooded.

Motion: a pulse travels the line and hits each attempt. The growing gaps between attempts are drawn to scale, so exponential backoff is visible, not just described.

plate 07 · reconciliation

Our books, their report, side by side

Every night Zerodrift lines up its ledger against the gateway's settlement file. Anything that doesn't match gets flagged, never silently fixed.
